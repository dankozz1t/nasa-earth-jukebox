# Listen without installing

Three pieces rendered by Earth Jukebox, so you can hear the result before running the app. MP3, 128 kbps, stereo. Use headphones: on the live side the north plays in the left ear and the south in the right.

| File | Record | Data | Settings |
|---|---|---|---|
| [four-aerosols.mp3](four-aerosols.mp3) | Four aerosols (NASA archive) | NASA SVS 5669, GEOS aerosols, 19 Aug 2025, 20:00 UTC | harmony mode, 64 s, sensitivity 2, all four layers and the bed |
| [gentle-scale.mp3](gentle-scale.mp3) | Gentle scale (NASA archive) | same frame | scale mode (D Dorian), 96 s, sensitivity 1 |
| [rain-right-now.mp3](rain-right-now.mp3) | Rain right now (Live) | Open-Meteo precipitation, reading of 14 Nov 2026, 13:45 UTC | 3 waves, range 0.8, 60 s, rule-based composer |

In the NASA pieces you hear the map from west to east: black carbon is the smoky saw, dust the warm pad, sea salt the airy voice with surf, sulfate the bell. Higher notes mean the layer sits further north, louder means it covers more of the map. How it works: [../sound-mapping.md](../sound-mapping.md).

The same audio, plus MIDI, CSV, JSON and captions, comes out of the Save menu in the app.
